Mums Fundraiser

Here's your opportunity to raise funds for your scouting adventures and the Troop! We're selling 12" potted single-colored mums for $20 and 12" potted tri-colored mums for $25. The Troop receives $6 per single-colored mum and $8 per tri-colored mum.

Get your mum vouchers from Mrs. Youngers, then sell them to your customers — you collect the money, they keep the voucher(s). Starting September 23, customers take their vouchers to select and pick up their mums at Ayers Farm, 1209 Meridian Street, Huntsville, AL.

Bring your order form, collected money, and any unsold vouchers back to Mrs. Youngers. Profits go into your Troop Web Host account for your scouting adventures. Questions — see Mrs. Youngers.
